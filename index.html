Portfolio

  • Duckademy

    Drawing game for Apple TV

    Duckademy is a multiplayer tvOS exclusive game where two players use their iPhone devices as remote controllers in fast-paced agility duels. Created by students from Apple Developer Academy | PUC-Rio, the project was born from the challenge of redefining Apple TV, traditionally associated with streaming, as a powerful gaming console.

    Designed for shared play, it transforms the big screen into an attraction for diverse audiences, while exercising agility and fine motor coordination. Once downloaded, Apple TV becomes the gateway into Duckademy's world, where players embody one of Master Patoto's seven apprentices, competing for the title of Supreme Wizarduck.

    The experience blends narrative, competition, and real-time illustrated challenges. Every moment on the iPhone is mirrored instantly on the TV: with a single continuous stroke, players replicate runes displayed on screen with precision and speed. More than a game, Duckademy reimagines the living room as a shared, magical, and interactive play space.

  • Hope Squad

    iOS game to promote volunteering

    Hope Squad is a mobile game designed to make volunteering fun.

    Players compete in capture-the-flag matches against AI-controlled teams created by other players. To build and customize their teams, players collect mascots, each representing a partner NGO and offering unique abilities for gameplay. To level up mascots faster, players complete real-world missions that support their NGOs, such as making donations or promoting them on social media. As they collect and evolve their mascots, players learn more about the organizations, connect with their missions, and actively contribute to their positive impact on society.

  • Modulus

    Educational iPad game about Linear Algebra

    An educational iPad game focused on the fundamentals of linear algebra.

    Lost in a mysterious plane ruled by the laws of Mathematics, you need to unravel the secrets of Linear Algebra in order to find an escape. Joined by the legendary mathematician Gauss, you'll learn to manipulate vectors, throw magical orbs to modify coordinates, and channel the power of matrices to bend the space around you. In a three-minute experience, Modulus turns math into adventure, puts your mind to the challenge and shows abstract concepts with a visual and engaging approach. The learning process happens naturally, like an explorer coming across the hidden laws of Mathematics.

    Modulus was submitted for Apple's WWDC25 Swift Student Challenge, and was selected amongst the 350 winners worldwide.

  • Alfieri

    Data science project regarding chess player analysis

    A data science tool for chess players who want performance-based insights for a more targeted study plan. Alfieri retrieves the player's games from chess.com and uses Stockfish paired with machine learning models to identify the aspects with the biggest impact on game results.

  • Flavify

    Educational web application using NLP

    Flavify makes learning more engaging by turning studying into an interactive experience. The app presents open-ended questions, allowing users to practice their knowledge and receive immediate feedback on their answers. Using Natural Language Processing (NLP), Flavify compares each response with an expected answer based on its meaning, providing a score that helps users identify gaps in their understanding and track their progress.

    Beyond practicing questions, Flavify includes an administrative panel for creating, editing, and managing study content, making it easy to keep the learning experience relevant and up to date. Designed as a flexible study companion, it encourages users to learn at their own pace, strengthen their understanding, and build confidence through continuous practice.
